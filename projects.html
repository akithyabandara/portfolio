My Work

3D Animation

This specific animation was a university project done by Blender. The animation features K-VRC from love, death and robots. The robot is entirely modeled from Blender and the assets are imported from Sketchfab.

This assessment was my first attempt at 3D modelling, so I'm very proud of the outcome. The rendered images contain carefully placed lighting as well that plays in with the objects surrounding the robot and environmental phenomena.

Pixel Animation

Yes, I am a massive sci-fi nerd :D These pixel animations were done for yet another university project. The medium used was the software Aseprite. My intention was to represent Space from the inside out, starting from Earth, to our solar system, our galaxy and then finally the Universe! The process included animating movements frame by frame that taught me the basics of animation.
